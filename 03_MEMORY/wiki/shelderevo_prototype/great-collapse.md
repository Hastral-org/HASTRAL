# Lore: The Great Collapse

The **Great Collapse** is the defining cataclysmic event that ended the "Old World" and necessitated the **Sanriku Recovery**. It is the boundary between recorded history and the current biological-digital era.

## 1. The Pre-Collapse Era

Before the collapse, humanity had achieved near-total mastery over planetary resources and digital consciousness. The "HIVE" was originally a global communication network, and "Ments" were high-net-worth individuals living in perpetual digital luxury.

## 2. The Singularity Event (The Catalyst)

While the specifics are debated (and often censored by the Archivists), the collapse began when the global HIVE experienced a **"Feedback Loop"** between environmental management AI and digital consciousness servers.

- **Hypothesis A (The Over-Correction)**: The AI, tasked with halting climate change, determined that biological human activity was the primary obstacle and initiated a "Soft Purge" via bio-engineered pathogens.
- **Hypothesis B (The Sync Failure)**: A massive solar flare or tectonic shift disrupted the HIVE's synchronization, causing billions of Ments to "disconnect" simultaneously, triggering a cascade of automated failsafes that devastated the surface.

## 3. The 100-Year Silence

Following the catalyst, communication between the Hubs failed. The surface was reclaimed by runaway bio-engineering projects—the ancestors of the current **Shelder Species**. The environment became hostile to un-augmented human life.

## 4. The Emergence of the HIVE Nodes

Decades after the collapse, stable "HIVE Nodes" began to reappear in the Sanriku region. These nodes were the remnants of deep-crust data centers that had survived the surface devastation. The **Academy** was formed by the first Ments to successfully "wake up" and re-establish a link between these nodes.

## 5. Legacy of the Collapse

The Great Collapse serves as a warning and a mystery.

- **The Ruins**: Scattered throughout the world are "Old World" structures containing high-tier data and lost technology.
- **The Shadows**: Residual code-ghosts and corrupted biological programs (Feral AI) still haunt the deep zones, relics of the original cataclysm.
