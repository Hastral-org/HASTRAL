# Lore: The Digitized Ment

In the world of the *Sanriku Recovery*, players do not exist as physical humans. Instead, they are **Digitized Ments** (Mentalities)—uploaded consciousnesses serving the Academy.

## 1. The Nature of Ments

A Ment is a high-fidelity digital construct of a human mind. They are stored within the **HIVE Sync-Layer** and "downloaded" into biological vessels (Shelders) to perform Outreach missions.

## 2. Inhabitation (The Pilot Role)

The process of a Ment entering a Shelder is called **Inhabitation**.

- **The Sync Bridge**: The quality of the inhabitation is governed by the **S4 Affinity** level.
- **Data Harvesting**: A Ment's primary purpose is to act as a sensory probe, gathering biological data and experiences (`exps`) that the HIVE cannot generate on its own.

## 3. The Digital Existence

When not inhabiting a Shelder, Ments reside in the **Academy Hubs**. This is a purely digital environment where they interact with other Ments, trade data, and plan future missions.

- **Ment Decay**: Without regular "grounding" in a biological vessel, digital consciousness begins to drift (Degradation). This is the narrative driver for players needing to constantly return to the field.

## 4. POV & Interface

The game's UI (User Interface) is narratively explained as the Ment's **Digital Overlay**.

- **AR View**: Health bars, Biomass counters, and DNA markers are "Augmented Reality" projections piped directly into the Ment's visual cortex.
- **HIVE Comms**: The chat and quest systems are direct neural links to Academy Command.

## 5. Death & Redundancy

Because Ments are digital, "Death" in a Shelder is not the end for the Ment—only for the vessel.

- **Backup Restoration**: Upon vessel death, the Ment is "snapped back" to the HIVE. However, the trauma of the disconnect often results in the loss of recent `exps` and a total reset of the **S4 Affinity** with that specific organism.
