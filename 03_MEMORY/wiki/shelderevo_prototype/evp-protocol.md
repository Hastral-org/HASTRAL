# Concept: Evolving Value Protocol (EVP)

The **Evolving Value Protocol (EVP)** is the "Cold Storage" currency of Shelder Evolution. While **Biomass** is volatile and tied to a physical body, EVP is tied to the player's **Digitized Mind** and persists across character lifecycles.

## 1. Philosophy: Value Preservation

In a Hard Sci-Fi setting where bodies are disposable but data is immortal, EVP represents the "data-wealth" accumulated by an individual. It solves the psychological pain of permadeath by ensuring that no effort is ever truly lost.

## 2. Acquisition Methods

EVP cannot be mined or found; it is strictly **earned** through the biological cycle:

- **Post-Mortem Conversion**: When a Shelder dies, its accumulated experience, unique mutations, and total biomass throughput are calculated and converted into EVP.
- **Scientific Contributions**: Providing biological samples or research data to the **Academy** (Hastal NPCs) generates EVP.
- **System Maintenance**: Performing tasks that stabilize the planet's ecosystem or the HIVE network.

## 3. The "Legacy" Spending Loop

EVP is used for meta-progression that outlives any single creature:

| Expenditure Category   | Description                                                                  |
|------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Genetic Blueprints** | Unlocking higher-tier species or rare mutations for the next respawn.        |
| **Academy Access**     | Purchasing permanent skill-ups or knowledge modules for the player's "Mind." |
| **Clout & Governance** | Voting on server-wide events, ecosystem changes, or legislation.             |
| **HIVE Assets**        | Investing in persistent infrastructure (hubs, caches, beacons).              |

## 4. Technical Implementation

In the Shelder Evolution architecture:

- **EVP** is stored in the **Global Database** (synchronized via the MAGPIE_Server).
- Unlike character-local stats (Stamina/Reserve), EVP transactions require a validated sync with the Discord-based database to prevent duplication or loss during a local crash.

## 5. Economic Stability

To prevent inflation, EVP follows a "Proof-of-Life" (or rather, "Proof-of-Death") model. The difficulty of earning EVP increases as the player's overall "Legacy" grows, ensuring that late-game progress remains challenging and meaningful.
