# Lore: The Shelder Species

The **Shelder** is the apex specimen of speculative biology in the Sanriku Recovery zone. They are not merely animals, but biological substrates designed for the hosting of **Digitized Minds**.

## 1. Biological Origins

Shelders are hypothesized to be the result of extreme convergent evolution or intentional genetic engineering by pre-collapse civilizations. They possess a unique "Neural Interface" organ at the base of the skull, allowing for the direct integration of digital consciousness.

## 2. Physiological Characteristics

- **Modular Morphology**: Shelders exhibit high phenotypic plasticity. Their DNA is highly "unstable" in its default state, allowing for rapid mutations triggered by environmental stressors or intentional EVP-spent upgrades.
- **Biomass Metabolism**: Their digestive system is capable of breaking down almost any organic matter into high-density energy (Reserve), though they have specific dietary requirements to maintain peak Stamina.
- **The "Sheath" (Shell)**: Most Shelders possess some form of chitinous or mineralized exterior (hence the name), providing protection against the harsh atmospheric conditions of the Sanriku zone.

## 3. The Evolutionary Path

Every Shelder starts as a "Null" specimen—a basic biological template. Through the consumption of Biomass and the accumulation of Experience, the species can branch into specialized roles:

- **Dromaaeosaur-Types**: Focused on Agility (DEX) and precision hunting.
- **Titan-Types**: Focused on Mass and endurance, often used for heavy lifting or siege operations.
- **Sensory-Types**: Specialized in HIVE signal reception and environmental detection.

## 4. The Mind-Body Connection

A Shelder without a Digitized Mind is a "Feral"—a creature of pure instinct and high aggression. When a player "inhabits" a Shelder, the creature gains the intelligence and strategic capacity of the player, but remains bound by the physical constraints of the **Fatigue-Stamina-Reserve** system.

## 5. Death and Continuity

When a Shelder body fails (Permadeath), the Digitized Mind is "extracted" back to the HIVE. The body remains as a "Biomass Cache" for other creatures to consume, while the player's experience is distilled into **EVP**, allowing for the "Assembly" of a newer, more evolved Shelder specimen.
