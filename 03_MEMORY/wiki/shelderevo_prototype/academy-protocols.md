# Lore: The Academy Protocols

The **Academy Protocols** are the standard operating procedures for **Digitized Ments** during their service in the *Sanriku Recovery*. They define the lifecycle of a mission and the rules of engagement with the ecosystem.

## 1. Pre-Flight: Vessel Selection

Before an "Outreach" mission, a Ment must select or be assigned a **Shelder Vessel**.

- **Foundational DNA**: The Academy provides basic genetic templates.
- **Legacy Seeding**: If a Ment has salvaged DNA from a previous successful mission, it can be used to "custom-print" a vessel with specific traits.

## 2. Phase 1: Inhabitation & Syncing

Upon deployment, the Ment undergoes the **Syncing Phase**.

- **Agency S0 -> S1**: The vessel wakes up.
- **The First Bond**: The Ment must perform immediate survival tasks (Feeding, Navigating) to stabilize the **S4 Affinity** link.

## 3. Phase 2: Data Acquisition (The Hunt)

The core objective of any mission is the gathering of **Experience Packets (`exps`)** and **Biomass**.

- **Experience Mapping**: Exploring new zones, interacting with flora/fauna, and surviving combat generates `exps`.
- **Biomass Collection**: Consuming biological matter to maintain the vessel's integrity and prepare for evolution.

## 4. Phase 3: Directed Evolution

When the vessel reaches a **HIVE Node** (or a stable camp), the Ment can initiate an Evolution event.

- **EVP Expenditure**: Spending gathered Evolution Potential to mutate the DNA.
- **The Chrysalis**: A period of vulnerability where the vessel is transformed.

## 5. Phase 4: Extraction & Backup

A mission ends when the Ment reaches an **Extraction Point** or the vessel reaches its biological limit.

- **Data Upload**: All gathered `exps` and DNA mutations are uploaded to the HIVE.
- **Ment Snap-Back**: The digital consciousness returns to the Academy Hub for debriefing and research.

## 6. Emergency Protocols (Permadeath)

If a vessel is destroyed before extraction:

- **Protocol 'Black-Box'**: The Ment is forcibly extracted. DNA data is corrupted (reset), and the S4 bond is severed.
- **The Penalty**: Loss of all un-uploaded `exps`.
