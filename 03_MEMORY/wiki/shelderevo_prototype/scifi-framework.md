# Doctrine: Hard Sci-Fi Realism Framework

Shelder Evolution is committed to "Hard" Science Fiction. This means that while we allow for speculative biology and future technologies, they must be grounded in physical laws and logical consistency.

## 1. The Core Directive

If a mechanic or lore element contradicts known physics without a "Hard" speculative explanation (e.g., "Magic"), it must be redesigned.

## 2. Biological Constraints

Creatures in Shelder Evolution are biological machines, not magical entities.

- **Thermal Management**: Large creatures must deal with heat dissipation. High exertion (Stamina use) increases body temperature.
- **Nutrient Density**: The Biomass Economy is a zero-sum game of calories and nutrients. A creature's size and capability are limited by what it can realistically consume.
- **Gravity & Locomotion**: Movement must respect mass, momentum, and the skeletal-muscular limits of the creature's design.

## 3. Technology & The "Digital Mind"

- **Digital Immortality**: Ments are digitalized, but they are not omniscient. They are limited by the bandwidth of their host bodies and the latency of the HIVE network.
- **The HIVE**: Not a magical "Cloud," but a network of physical beacons and relays (Discord-synced hubs). Signal strength and interference are real gameplay factors.

## 4. Mechanical Integration

Realism should enhance gameplay, not hinder it:

- **Fatigue as Metabolism**: Instead of "Poison" or "Mana Burn," use "Acidosis" or "Metabolic Collapse" as status effects.
- **Permadeath as Conservation**: Death is not an arbitrary penalty, but the recycling of matter (Biomass) and the preservation of data (EVP).

## 5. Design Checklist for New Features

1. **Can this be explained by biology or physics?**
2. **Does it follow the law of conservation of energy?**
3. **Is the complexity "necessary" or just "noise"?** (Hard Sci-Fi should be deep, not just difficult).
4. **Does it fit the "Sanriku Recovery" context?** (Post-disaster, resource-scarce, highly technical).

---
*By adhering to this framework, we ensure that Shelder Evolution remains a unique, high-fidelity experience that rewards players for understanding and mastering the world's underlying logic.*
