# Lore: The Sanriku Factions

The *Sanriku Recovery* is not a unified effort. While the Academy provides the overarching structure, internal ideologies differ on how humanity should reintegrate with the new biological world.

## 1. The Archivists (Pro-Tech)
The Archivists believe that the **Great Collapse** was a temporary setback and that humanity's true future lies in reclaiming its digital and technological peak.
- **Goal**: Full digitization of the ecosystem. They view Shelders as temporary tools or "Bio-Hardware."
- **Focus**: High-tier HIVE sync, advanced data harvesting, and "clean" genetic lines.
- **Philosophy**: "Evolution is a bug we must patch."

## 2. The Symbiotes (Naturalists)
The Symbiotes argue that the Collapse was a direct result of over-reliance on digital life. They believe humanity must evolve *with* the planet, not just inhabit it.
- **Goal**: Permanent biological reintegration. They seek to turn "Ments" back into physical, evolving organisms.
- **Focus**: High-affinity S4 bonds, wild mutation paths, and ecosystem stability.
- **Philosophy**: "The HIVE is a womb, not a home."

## 3. The Scavengers (Opportunists)
The Scavengers are less concerned with philosophy and more with survival and profit. They are the frontline Ments who take the highest-risk missions for the most EVP.
- **Goal**: Resource accumulation and personal power.
- **Focus**: Combat efficiency, fast evolution cycles, and exploiting "HIVE Shadows."
- **Philosophy**: "Biomass is the only truth."

## 4. Factional Tension in Gameplay
- **Quest Lines**: Different factions offer unique "Doctrine" skills and genetic templates.
- **Affinity Impact**: Symbiotes may require higher Affinity thresholds for certain upgrades, while Archivists may offer "Interface Boosts" that bypass some biological needs at the cost of higher Fatigue.
- **The Academy Balance**: The player's actions shift the balance of power between these groups, influencing the "Official" direction of the Recovery effort.
