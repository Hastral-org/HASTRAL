# Lore: The Ecosystem Map

The *Sanriku Recovery* zone is a geographically diverse region, divided into several distinct "Tiers" of stability, biological density, and HIVE signal strength.

## 1. Tier 0: The Academy Hubs

The "Green Zones." Highly stabilized environments protected by massive HIVE shielding.

- **Environment**: Managed gardens, research labs, and Ment digital-uplink stations.
- **Safety**: No feral predators. S4 Agency is the norm.
- **Role**: Onboarding, quest distribution, and DNA banking.

## 2. Tier 1: The Outreach (The Frontier)

The primary gameplay area for early-to-mid-stage missions.

- **Environment**: Overgrown forests, reclaimed coastal towns, and stable grasslands.
- **Safety**: Moderate biological threats. High density of "Exp" nodes.
- **HIVE Status**: Reliable signal. S2-S3 Agency common.

## 3. Tier 2: The Deep Sanriku (The Wilds)

Extreme environments where the **Great Collapse** left permanent scars.

- **Environment**: Bioluminescent jungles, irradiated canyons, and tectonic rift zones.
- **Safety**: High-threat apex predators. Rapid Biomass decay.
- **HIVE Status**: Intermittent signal. Creatures often fall back to S0-S1 (Feral) behavior.

## 4. Tier 3: The Shadow Zones (HIVE Blackouts)

Areas where the HIVE signal is completely absent or blocked.

- **Environment**: Underground bunkers, deep-sea trenches, or magnetically unstable peaks.
- **Safety**: Extreme danger. No "Respawn" or "Extraction" possible without manual intervention.
- **Lore**: Rumored locations of "Old World" sentient AIs and original HIVE source code.

## 5. The Migratory Corridors

The "Highways" of the ecosystem.

- **Nature**: Paths used by massive Shelder herds moving between Tier 1 and Tier 2.
- **Strategic Value**: High Biomass gathering potential but carries the risk of being caught in a "Stampede."
