# Lore: The Sanriku Recovery

The **Sanriku Recovery** is the historical and environmental context for the Shelder Evolution project. It describes a post-disaster, post-relativistic world where technology and biology have intertwined to survive a collapsed ecosystem.

## 1. The Cataclysm

While the exact details are obscured by time and data-loss, the Sanriku zone was the epicenter of a massive environmental and technological collapse (hypothesized to be an AI containment failure or a tectonic-biohazard event).

## 2. The Zones of Recovery

The world is divided into various sectors, each with its own level of stability and HIVE signal strength:

- **The Hubs**: Safe zones (like the Academy) where the HIVE signal is strong and the environment is stabilized.
- **The Outreach**: The frontier where players (Digitized Ments) "inhabit" Shelders to gather data and biomass.
- **The Deep Sanriku**: High-danger areas with extreme biological mutations and erratic HIVE signals.

## 3. The Academy

The **Academy** is the primary institution overseeing the recovery effort. It serves as:

- **Onboarding Center**: Where Ments are prepared for "Outreach" missions.
- **Research Hub**: Where Biomass and EVP are exchanged for genetic advancements.
- **Lore Role**: The Academy represents the "Pro-Tech" and "Naturalist" factions' attempt to restore the ecosystem via managed evolution.

## 4. The Digital Divide

A core theme of the recovery is the tension between the **Digitized Minds** (the players/Hastal staff) and the **Feral World**. The goal of the "Recovery" is not just to survive, but to reintegrate human-level consciousness into the planet's new biological reality.

## 5. Environmental Hazards

- **HIVE Shadows**: Areas where the sync-layer is blocked, leading to "S0-S1" only behavior (Creatures lose their player-driven agency).
- **Metabolic Rot**: High-radiation or chemically unstable zones that accelerate **Fatigue** and **Biomass** decay.
