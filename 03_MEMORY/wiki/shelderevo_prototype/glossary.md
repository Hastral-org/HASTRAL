# Shelder Evolution: Glossary of Terms

A quick reference for the terminology, acronyms, and jargon used in the *Shelder Evolution* project and the *Sanriku Recovery*.

## Core Systems

- **Biomass**: The primary biological resource used for sustenance, growth, and evolution.
- **DNA (Genome)**: The numeric representation of a creature's physical and behavioral traits.
- **EVP (Evolution Potential)**: Experience points gathered through survival and interaction, spent to trigger DNA mutations.
- **Fatigue**: A measure of biological stress. High fatigue leads to metabolic failure and death.
- **HIVE**: The global synchronization and data network that manages the Sanriku Recovery.
- **MAGPIE**: The state engine and data architecture that manages the HIVE's persistent world simulation.

## Agency & Consciousness

- **Agency Levels (S0-S4)**: The 5-tier classification of creature autonomy, from raw persistence (S0) to human-AI affinity (S4).
- **Ment (Mentality)**: A digitized human consciousness serving as a "pilot" for biological vessels.
- **Inhabitation**: The process of a Ment syncing with and controlling a Shelder vessel.
- **Affinity**: The strength of the bond between a Ment and their creature, influencing control responsiveness.
- **Exps (Experience Packets)**: Discrete units of raw sensory data and memories gathered by Ments for the HIVE.

## Entities & Environment

- **Shelder**: The primary biological organism of the Sanriku Recovery. A modular, evolving creature inhabited by Ments.
- **Sanriku Recovery**: The historical and environmental effort to restore the ecosystem following the Great Collapse.
- **HIVE Node**: A localized data terminal and safe zone used for extraction, evolution, and communication.
- **Shadow Zone**: An area with poor HIVE signal strength where creature agency is restricted to S0-S1.

## Factions

- **Archivists**: A faction focused on reclaiming "Old World" technology and total digitization.
- **Symbiotes**: A faction focused on biological reintegration and permanent evolution with the planet.
- **Scavengers**: Opportunistic Ments focused on resource gathering and survival at any cost.
